Notation key
Write Means Try
NdX N dice with X sides (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 20, 100); N defaults to 1
d% dF Percentile die; Fudge die (−1, 0, +1)
dc d2 Coin (0 tails, 1 heads); two-sided die (1, 2)
+ - numbers Add or subtract dice and constants
khN klN Keep the highest or lowest N dice
dlN dhN Drop the lowest or highest N dice
kN dN Short for khN and dlN
! x Explode: roll again on the maximum
!>=5 !6 Explode on a comparison
r r1 r<3 Reroll matching dice until they stop matching (default: 1s)
ro ro1 Reroll matching dice once
>=7 <3 =6 Count successes among the kept dice
adv dis Advantage and disadvantage: 2d20kh1, 2d20kl1

Ability scores: 4d6dl1 rolls four d6 and drops the lowest (same as 4d6kh3). Case and spaces don't matter.
